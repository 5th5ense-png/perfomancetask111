Accommodation Guide

Choose a convenient base for your Chongqing adventure.

一 PREMIUM STAYS

Luxury Hotels

Ideal for travelers seeking upscale rooms, dining, concierge services, and city or river views. Compare locations and facilities before booking.

二 COMFORT

Mid-Range Hotels

A practical choice for families and tourists who want private rooms, comfortable amenities, and convenient access to transport.

三 BUDGET FRIENDLY

Hostels & Guesthouses

Consider budget stays for shorter visits or meeting fellow travelers. Check recent reviews, room conditions, and guest registration rules.

Where Should You Stay?

Jiefangbei: convenient for shopping, dining, and exploring central attractions.

Near major metro stations: useful for reaching attractions across the city.

Nanbin Road: worth considering for riverfront scenery and views of the illuminated skyline.

Booking Checklist

  • Check the exact address and nearest metro station.
  • Confirm check-in times, cancellation rules, and total cost.
  • Verify that the property can legally accommodate international visitors.
  • Read recent reviews and confirm the booking directly with the provider.
