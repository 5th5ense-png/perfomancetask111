A TASTE OF CHONGQING

Local Food & Delicacies

Bold spices, rich broths, and unforgettable street food.

一 CITY FAVORITE

Chongqing Hotpot

A bubbling broth often rich in chili oil, Sichuan peppercorns, garlic, and spices. Diners cook thin slices of meat, vegetables, tofu, and noodles at the table.

二 COMFORT FOOD

Chongqing Noodles

Often known as xiaomian, these noodles are commonly served with chili oil, preserved vegetables, garlic, and other savory seasonings.

三 STREET FOOD

Street Snacks

Explore local markets for skewers, dumplings, spicy snacks, and sweet treats. Ask vendors about ingredients and spice levels.

Food Tips for Visitors

  • Tell restaurant staff if you prefer mild, medium, or very spicy food.
  • Try busy, well-maintained restaurants with high food turnover.
  • Carry water and consider ordering rice or a non-spicy dish if needed.
  • Ask about peanuts, sesame, seafood, and other allergens before ordering.
