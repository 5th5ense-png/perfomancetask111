GETTING AROUND THE CITY

Transportation & Travel Guide

Navigate Chongqing's unique network of railways, roads, and river routes.

Chongqing Rail Transit

The city's rail transit system connects many residential, commercial, and tourist districts. Some lines feature remarkable elevated and mountainous routes.

Taxis and Ride-Hailing

Taxis and ride-hailing services can be convenient for direct trips. Keep your destination written in Chinese for easier communication.

Walking

Walking is a great way to explore local streets, but steep slopes, staircases, and multi-level roads can make distances feel longer than expected.

River Cruises

Selected sightseeing and river travel services offer a different perspective on the Yangtze and the surrounding landscape. Check schedules in advance.

Essential Travel Tips

  • Check the latest visa and entry requirements before travelling to China.
  • Prepare a translation app and save your hotel address in Chinese.
  • Confirm payment options for public transport and taxis before departure.
  • Allow extra travel time because Chongqing has steep roads and complex interchanges.
  • Check current metro routes and timetables using official transit information.
